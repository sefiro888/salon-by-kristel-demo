Diagnosis first

In colour, every detail counts

My way of working is always based on listening, analysing and adapting. If you're thinking about a colour change, it's important to start from the diagnosis, not only from the idea.

Every highlights service comes with a treatment to restore the hair fibre: because a beautiful blonde starts with healthy hair.

  • Diagnosis appointment (€20) that is deducted from the next one
  • Salonlab Smart Analyzer scanner to get to know your hair
