How we work

A good appointment doesn't start with the scissors

It starts much earlier: listening, explaining, assessing and giving you your own space.

We listen and diagnose

We assess the real condition of your hair and what you want to achieve. If needed, the diagnosis appointment (€20) is deducted from the next one.

We propose with judgement

Before choosing a colour, we ask how much maintenance you want: the result has to fit your time and your routine.

We work with calm

Enough time for each service and professional products such as GOA Organics, Bondfinity or Scalpclinix.

We look after the result

A maintenance and care plan so your colour and cut keep looking great weeks later.
