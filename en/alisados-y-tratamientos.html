Analyzer diagnosis

Look first, then decide

Not all hair needs the same thing, and often what we see on the outside isn't enough.

With the Salonlab Smart Analyzer hair scanner we discover the unique quality of your hair and recommend personalised products. We start with an assessment appointment (€20), which is deducted from the next one.
