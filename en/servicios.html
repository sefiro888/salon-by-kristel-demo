Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Do I need an appointment?
Yes. The salon works by appointment only, to give every client all the time she needs. You can book online on Booksy or call 608 96 74 80.
What is the diagnosis appointment?
It's an appointment (€20, 20 min) to assess your next treatment or colour when it isn't clear what can be done with your hair's quality. The fee is deducted from your next appointment.
Why do highlights come with a treatment?
Every highlights service comes with a treatment to restore the hair fibre: beautiful colour starts with healthy hair.
What does “from” mean?
The final price depends on length, density and the work needed. When you see “from”, the exact amount is confirmed at the diagnosis.
Do you have gift cards or vouchers?
Gift cards and session vouchers are available on Booksy, plus a loyalty programme.
Can I bring children or pets?
Yes: the salon is child-friendly, pets are allowed and there is easy access for people with reduced mobility.
