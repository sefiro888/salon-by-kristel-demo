Beautiful hair all year round: the keys

Beautiful hair isn't about making it perfect, it's about caring for it consistently.

Book an appointment More tips

Having beautiful hair isn't about making it perfect, it's about caring for it consistently with products that suit you. These are the keys Kristel always recommends.

01

Maintenance cuts

It's advisable to trim every 2 or 3 months to clear away any split ends.

02

Hydration suited to your hair type

A deep hydration treatment once a month at the salon is recommended.

03

Always use heat protection

There are several textures so you can choose the one that suits you best: cream, serum, spray…

04

Fewer products, better chosen

You don't need lots of products for your daily routine: just the right ones.

A summary of the tips Kristel publishes on her Instagram (translated). The short explanations are for guidance: every head of hair is different.
